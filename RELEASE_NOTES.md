One Quick Access tab in Steam Big Picture for the panels of all your [Millennium](https://steambrew.app) plugins.

## Highlights

- The plug tab is part of Steam's own Quick Access menu from its first render.
- Every Millennium plugin with a panel is listed as soon as it loads, including its title buttons.
- Panels fit the Quick Access width, and dropdown menus stay fully on screen.
- Default game page tab: game pages open straight on Activity, Your Stuff, Community or Game Info.

## Install

1. Download `plugins-hub.star` below.
2. Put it in `Steam\millennium\plugins\`.
3. Restart Steam and enable Plugins Hub in Millennium's plugin settings.

## Requirements

- A Starlight-capable Millennium (verified with v3.6.2).
- Steam Big Picture mode.
